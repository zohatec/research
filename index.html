About ARC

The ARC is a premier research organization dedicated to advancing medical research and building research capacity in Bangladesh. Our mission is to guide researchers, students, and professionals through every stage of the research process.

Since our establishment, we have trained thousands of researchers, published hundreds of papers, and provided consultancy services to institutions across the country. Our team of experienced professors and researchers brings decades of expertise in public health, clinical research, and research methodology.
