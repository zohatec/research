About ARC

Guiding Research, Building Expertise – since 2019

🎯

Our Mission

To empower researchers, students, and health professionals with practical skills, ethical guidance, and expert support – from protocol writing to publication.

🌟

Our Vision

A thriving research ecosystem in Bangladesh where every researcher has access to quality mentorship, training, and resources to produce impactful, evidence‑based work.

Our Story

ARC began as a small initiative by a group of passionate researchers at the University of Rajshahi. Recognising the gap between academic knowledge and practical research execution, we started offering informal workshops on research methodology. Over the years, with the support of experienced faculty members and healthcare professionals, ARC has grown into a trusted organisation that has trained over 500 researchers, supported more than 200 publications, and provided consultancy to hundreds of postgraduate students and clinicians. Today, we continue to expand our reach through online courses, free resources, and one‑on‑one mentorship.
