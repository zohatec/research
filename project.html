Our Research Projects

Collaborative initiatives that drive evidence‑based change

📋
Ongoing

Strengthening Institutional Review Board (IRB) Capacity in Rajshahi

A collaborative project with the University of Rajshahi to train IRB members, streamline ethics application processes, and develop standard operating procedures for human subject research.

📅 Started: Jan 2026👥 Partners: RU, BMRC
🩺
Ongoing

Prevalence of Hypertension & Diabetes Among Rural Adults

A community‑based cross‑sectional study covering five villages in Rajshahi district. Data collection includes anthropometric measurements, blood pressure, and random glucose screening.

📅 Started: Mar 2026👥 Partners: Community Clinics
📊
Completed

Systematic Review on Mobile Health Interventions for Diabetes Self‑Management

A meta‑analysis of 28 randomised controlled trials evaluating the effectiveness of SMS‑based and app‑based interventions. Findings published in the Journal of Medical Internet Research.

📅 Completed: Dec 2025📄 Publication: JMIR 2026
📚
Completed

Developing a Bangla Research Ethics Training Module

In collaboration with the Bangladesh Bioethics Society, we created a free, open‑access training module on research ethics for medical students and early‑career researchers.

📅 Completed: Aug 2025🌐 Available: ARC E‑Library

Ongoing

Predictors of Publication Success Among Bangladeshi Medical Students

A mixed‑methods study exploring barriers and facilitators to publishing research. Survey of 500+ students with in‑depth interviews of successful early‑career authors.

📅 Started: Feb 2026📊 Expected completion: Dec 2026

Completed

ARC Online SPSS Workshop Series

A series of 10 live online workshops reaching 1,200+ participants from 30+ institutions. Recordings and materials are now available for free on our YouTube channel.
